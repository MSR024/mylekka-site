The question

“Does my spending reflect what truly matters to me?”

Most of us know how much we earn, but few truly understand where our money goes, why we spend it, and whether it reflects what matters to us. MyLekka is built around this one question.

Beyond tracking

It’s not just what you spend

Most apps stop at what you spent and where it went. MyLekka goes further and asks why — because that’s what actually helps you understand your own spending.

What
What you actually spend on, laid out plainly.
Where
Where that money goes — the places, people and patterns behind it.
How
How it adds up, and how it shapes your life over time.
₹

Why is the part that matters most — the reason behind each entry, and the pattern that becomes visible once you see it.

See the why often enough, and understanding your own spending stops being work. You simply start learning from it.

What matters

The goal isn’t simply to spend less or save more. It’s to align your spending with what matters — family, health, education, experiences and your future.

Because real wealth isn’t just how much money we have. It’s how well we use it for the people and life that matter to us.

As India grows, earns and spends more, we want that growth to show up as better choices and better lives — not just bigger numbers.
